GSM Lift Intercom

The GSM Lift Intercom is specifically designed for emergency two-way communication on elevators.

Upon activating the Emergency Alarm button in the elevator, the GSM lift Intercom automatically performs the following functions:
  • A pre-recorded message notifies passengers that their Emergency has been received.
  • The GSM Lift Intercom automatically dials the pre-programmed emergency number and enables full two-way communication. The trapped person can communicate directly to the 24-hour call centre.
  • The GSM Lift Intercom also sends a SMS to a pre-programmed cell phone number informing the owner/building manager of the emergency.
  • Anyone that has the GSM Lift Intercom cell phone number can contact the elevator, check on the occupants and inform them on the progress.
Key Feature
  • No direct interaction needed between occupants and GSM lift Intercom. Only push standard lift alarm button.
  • Installation of GSM Lift Intercom requires no major cosmetic or structural changes to the elevator.
  • GSM Lift Intercom has a build-in standby battery that lasts up to 5 days.
  • GSM Lift Intercom is compatible to all makes of elevators.
  • Downtime for installation less than 3 hours.
  • Operates on all cellular networks, pre-paid or contract SIM cards.
  • Reduces downtime of elevator and quick response due to direct line of communication with call centre.
  • Complies with Lift regulation SANS 50081-1-clause 14.2.3.3 Elevator Emergency communication system (Basic requirements)
  • 12 Months warrantee on all components.
